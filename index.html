01 — About me

I'm a Software Engineer focused on automating processes, designing scalable data models and streamlining system integrations — delivering solutions that improve efficiency, accuracy and business decision-making.

Most of my work lives in the space between systems: an ERP that needs to talk to a storefront, a CRM that needs clean data, a warehouse that needs a reliable nightly pipeline. I design the contracts, build the integrations and make sure they fail loudly instead of silently.

I care about observable systems, sensible data models and documentation that outlives the project. If it can be automated safely, it should be.

04 — Process

How the work happens

A predictable four-step rhythm. You always know what's being built, why, and what comes next.

STEP 01

Discover & map

We walk the current process end to end — systems, data owners, edge cases and the things that break today. Output is a written map, not a guess.

STEP 02

Design the contract

Data models, field mappings, error strategy and sync direction get agreed before a single line of code. This is where most integration projects are won or lost.

STEP 03

Build & validate

Incremental delivery in a sandbox, with real data samples. You see working flows early and often instead of one big reveal at the end.

STEP 04

Ship & watch

Go-live with monitoring, alerting and runbooks, followed by a hypercare window so nothing fails quietly on day three.
