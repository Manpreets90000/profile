About

I automate processes, design scalable data models and streamline system integrations.

Most of my work lives in the space between systems: an ERP that needs to talk to a storefront, a CRM that needs clean data, a warehouse that needs a reliable nightly pipeline. I design the contracts, build the integrations and make sure they fail loudly instead of silently.

I care about observable systems, sensible data models and documentation that outlives the project. If it can be automated safely, it should be.
